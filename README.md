The surprise is the reversal — the shadow leads, I follow.

## Why this angle

A shadow is the most ordinary thing on a street, and it always follows. Here it arrives first — half a step ahead, already in a hurry — so the walker becomes the follower. Nothing supernatural happens; the wonder is that the familiar order quietly flips, and the street you had not chosen yet is already being crossed for you.
